# Governance

Open Detection Engineering is currently a community project in its beginning stage. Governance is intentionally lightweight and will evolve as the contributor community grows.

## Principles

ODE governance should:
- preserve open access to the curriculum
- prioritize technical quality
- encourage broad practitioner participation
- prevent any organization, including the project's steward, from controlling curriculum content for commercial reasons
- recognize sustained contributors
- keep decision-making transparent
- remain as simple as practical

## Roles

### Project Lead

Responsible for overall project direction, governance, and day-to-day decisions, following the [Review Process](docs/REVIEW-PROCESS.md).

The Project Lead is appointed by HardCounter LLC and serves until they step down or HardCounter replaces them. The Project Lead makes content decisions on technical grounds, in public. A change of Project Lead is a major change: it is announced and discussed in the open before it takes effect.

### Maintainers

Trusted contributors with responsibility for reviewing and merging contributions, managing project areas, and helping guide curriculum direction.

### Domain Reviewers

Subject-matter experts who review material within specific technical areas.

Reviewers do not necessarily have repository write access.

### Contributors

Anyone whose accepted work improves the project.

Org membership is not required to contribute.

## Decisions

Routine changes may be approved by the appropriate maintainer.

Major changes should be discussed publicly through GitHub issues or pull requests before adoption.

Examples include:
- restructuring Core
- adding or removing major tracks
- significant governance changes, including changes to the steward's role or the trademark policy
- licensing changes
- major shifts in project scope

## Stewardship and Project Assets

HardCounter LLC stewards ODE. It owns the ODE name and marks (governed by [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md)), hosts the repository and project infrastructure, and provides administrative and legal support.

HardCounter does not decide curriculum content. Content decisions follow this document and the [Review Process](docs/REVIEW-PROCESS.md), and an approach is not excluded because it conflicts with HardCounter's commercial interests. Where legitimate approaches differ, the curriculum presents the tradeoffs.

Curriculum content is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 and code under Apache License 2.0 ([LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)). Anyone may use, adapt, and redistribute it, including commercially, under those terms.

## Commercial Organizations

Commercial organizations are encouraged to contribute. Sponsorship, partnership, or financial support does **not** grant editorial control.

Vendor specific contributions should be reviewed by independent practitioners where practical.

HardCounter, as steward, may offer commercial products and services based on ODE, such as live training cohorts. These receive no editorial influence over the curriculum and follow the same licensing, trademark, disclosure, and review rules as everyone else's.

Maintainers and reviewers disclose affiliations as described in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) and recuse themselves from approving content that materially concerns their own or their employer's products.

## Evolution

As ODE grows, governance may evolve toward a broader community led structure, including additional maintainers and working groups.
