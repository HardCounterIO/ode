## What support does and does not mean

Support is appreciated, but it does **not** grant editorial control, influence over curriculum, review privileges, or any form of endorsement. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#commercial-organizations).

Supporters are listed alphabetically. Listing is not an endorsement of any supporter's products or services by ODE, nor of ODE by the supporter.

## Removal

A supporter may ask to be removed at any time, and ODE may remove a listing if the supporter violates the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
