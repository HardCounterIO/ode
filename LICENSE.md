## Using ODE Commercially

Training providers, vendors, and other organizations may use ODE in paid products and services. In practice:

**Allowed without asking, under any license or business model:**
- Teaching ODE curriculum, unmodified, in a paid course, with attribution.
- Building your own labs, exercises, assessments, videos, and platform around the curriculum, as long as they are your own original work and not rewrites of ODE text.
- Using ODE lab tooling (Apache 2.0) in proprietary lab infrastructure, following the Apache 2.0 terms.
- Charging for instruction, mentorship, certification of your own program, hosting, or delivery.

**Required:**
- Attribution: credit ODE, link to the source, link to the license, and indicate if you changed anything.
- If you modify ODE educational content (rewriting, translating, condensing, or remixing lessons or written lab instructions) and distribute the result, you must release your modified version under CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible license.

**Examples:**

| Scenario | ShareAlike applies? |
|---|---|
| Course slides that cite and link to ODE lessons, plus your own original labs | No |
| A paid course that includes ODE lessons unmodified, with attribution | No, but the ODE lessons stay CC BY-SA |
| Your own lab environment that implements an ODE learning objective | No |
| A rewritten or condensed version of an ODE lesson in your course materials | Yes, your version must be CC BY-SA |
| A translated copy of an ODE module | Yes |
| Proprietary lab tooling built on ODE's Apache 2.0 code | No |
