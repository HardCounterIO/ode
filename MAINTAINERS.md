## Affiliation Disclosure

Every Project Lead, maintainer, and domain reviewer must list in the table above:

- **Employer**, or "Independent."
- **Commercial interests** in anything related to ODE: training, labs, platforms, consulting, certification, or products competing in or built on the same space. Write "None" if there are none.

Rules:
- Update your entry within 30 days of any change.
- Undisclosed commercial interests are a Code of Conduct matter.
- Do not approve or merge content that materially concerns your own or your employer's products or services. Ask another maintainer or an independent reviewer.
- Disclosure is not a bar to participation. Its purpose is transparency.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Maintainer status is earned through sustained contribution and trust. Contributing once does not automatically grant repository permissions or organization membership.

As the project grows, maintainers may nominate additional maintainers or reviewers based on demonstrated participation and expertise.
